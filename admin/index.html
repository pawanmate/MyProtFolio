Publishing Workflow Summary

• Draft Mode: Any edits made across the sections are saved as a Draft in your database and are completely invisible to public visitors.
• Preview Mode: Click "Preview Draft" to view your portfolio with all draft modifications rendered live.
• Publishing: Click "Publish Live" to instantly make your draft changes public across the entire website without triggering any Vercel rebuilds or downtime.
